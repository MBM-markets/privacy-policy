Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 27, 2026

Application: MBM Commodity RR Calculator ("the app")

Operator: Money Badger Markets ("MBM", "we", "us")

1. Summary

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, receive, store, share or sell any personal data. The app works fully offline.

2. What Stays on Your Device

The numbers you type, your settings, your saved presets and your Trade Journal entries are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. Nothing is sent to any server or cloud service. Only you can see this data. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes your journal and settings. MBM cannot recover lost data, so please back up your journal regularly.

3. Export, Import and Backup

  • Export CSV saves a file to the Documents folder on your device, inside a folder named MBM. If the file cannot be saved to Documents, the app opens your device's share menu instead.
  • Share / Backup creates a file and hands it to your device's share menu. You choose where it goes (for example cloud storage, a messaging app or local files).
  • Import CSV reads a file that you select from your device.

We do not receive any exported or shared file. Where a shared file goes is your choice, and the service you pick has its own privacy policy.

4. Storage Permission

On Android 10 and older, the app may ask for storage permission so that Export CSV can save to the Documents folder. The permission is used only for the actions you start (Export, Share / Backup and Import). The app does not read, change or send any other files on your device.

5. Device Features

The app does not use your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.

6. Ads, Analytics and Third-Party Services

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools. It does not connect to third-party servers. The app is packaged with Capacitor, an open-source framework, and uses its Filesystem, Share and App plugins. These run only on your device and do not send data to us or to anyone else.

7. Fonts

The app includes the Inter and Share Tech Mono fonts inside the app itself. They are used under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. No font is loaded from the internet.

8. External Links

The About tab has links to MBM's public channels (YouTube, Instagram and Telegram). They open outside the app, in your browser or in the related app, only when you tap them. Those services have their own privacy policies.

9. Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

10. Financial Disclaimer

The app is a risk-reward, position-size and margin calculator for education and planning only. It does not place trades, hold money, give trading signals, predict profit, or give financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Leveraged products (Gold, Silver and PAXG) carry a high risk of rapid loss. Always check all numbers with your own broker or exchange before trading.

11. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
